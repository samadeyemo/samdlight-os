Customer Understanding

Customers Are More Than Names in a Contact List

A customer database should help a business understand relationships — not simply store phone numbers.

← Back to Blog

Another area many small businesses overlook is customer information.

A customer database should help a business understand relationships—not simply store phone numbers.

Useful customer information can include:

✓Purchase history
✓Outstanding balances
✓Transaction activity
✓Customer frequency
✓Customer value
✓Changes in buying behaviour

This creates an opportunity to move from:

“I think this customer hasn't bought from us recently.”

to:

“This customer has not purchased in the last several weeks.”

The difference is simple:

Guessing vs. knowing.

And better business decisions are usually made when there is evidence behind them.
