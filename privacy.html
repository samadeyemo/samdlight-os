Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

At SamDlight OS, we believe businesses should have greater clarity, control, and confidence over the information they use to operate and grow.

1. About SamDlight OS

SamDlight OS is an AI-powered Business Operating System designed to help African SMEs manage their business operations, understand their business data, and make better decisions with greater clarity.

SamDlight OS is operated by SamDlight Consulting Services, a digital transformation and business consulting company focused on helping businesses build stronger systems, structures, technology capabilities, and revenue operations.

Operating Company: SamDlight Consulting Services

Product: SamDlight OS

Product Focus: AI-powered business management, analytics, and business intelligence for SMEs

Primary Market: African businesses and SMEs

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, information generated through your use of SamDlight OS, and certain technical information collected automatically.

2.1 Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Login and authentication information
  • Account preferences
  • User role or business position
  • Subscription and account status

2.2 Business Information

  • Business name
  • Business type
  • Industry or sector
  • Business location
  • Country of operation
  • Business contact information
  • Business registration information where provided
  • Business size and operational information

2.3 Business and Financial Data

SamDlight OS is designed to help businesses manage their operations. Users may therefore choose to enter or upload information such as:

  • Sales transactions
  • Revenue records
  • Expenses
  • Inventory records
  • Products and services
  • Cost information
  • Profit and loss information
  • Customer information
  • Supplier information
  • Staff or team information
  • Business reports
  • Operational records
  • Business performance information
  • Other information voluntarily entered into a workspace

Important: Business owners and administrators are responsible for ensuring that they have the appropriate authority and lawful basis to provide personal information relating to customers, employees, suppliers, or other individuals to SamDlight OS.

2.4 Technical Information

We may automatically collect technical and usage information when you access SamDlight OS, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Login times
  • Pages and features accessed
  • Session information
  • Referring website
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Security and authentication logs

3. How We Use Information

We use information collected through SamDlight OS to provide, secure, maintain, and improve the platform.

  • Creating and managing accounts
  • Creating and managing business workspaces
  • Providing business management features
  • Processing business records and transactions
  • Generating dashboards and reports
  • Calculating business performance metrics
  • Providing analytics and business intelligence
  • Providing AI-assisted business insights
  • Providing customer support
  • Sending important service notifications
  • Processing subscriptions and payments
  • Detecting fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Maintaining platform security
  • Improving product performance and usability
  • Developing new features
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements

4. AI-Powered Features

SamDlight OS may provide AI-powered features that analyze information supplied by users to generate business insights, summaries, recommendations, classifications, or other business assistance.

AI outputs are intended to assist business users and should not automatically be treated as professional accounting, tax, legal, financial, medical, or other regulated professional advice. AI features do not independently take actions on a user's behalf or perform business automation.

Users remain responsible for reviewing important AI-generated information before relying on it for significant business decisions.

4.1 Business Data and AI Training

SamDlight OS does not intentionally use private business records submitted by customers to train publicly available AI models without appropriate authorization or disclosure.

Where third-party artificial intelligence providers are used to provide a specific feature, information may be processed by those providers only as reasonably necessary to provide that feature and subject to applicable contractual and privacy requirements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or private business records.

We may share information only where reasonably necessary for the operation of SamDlight OS or where permitted or required by law.

5.1 Technology and Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party providers for services such as:

  • Cloud hosting
  • Database infrastructure
  • Authentication
  • Email delivery
  • Analytics
  • Artificial intelligence services
  • Payment processing
  • Security monitoring
  • Customer support
  • Other software infrastructure

5.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law
  • Respond to lawful government requests
  • Comply with court orders or legal processes
  • Investigate fraud or security incidents
  • Protect our legal rights
  • Protect users or the public from harm

5.3 Business Transactions

If SamDlight Consulting Services or SamDlight OS becomes involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, relevant information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

6. Business Workspaces and User Permissions

SamDlight OS may allow business owners and administrators to invite employees, staff members, partners, or other authorized individuals into a business workspace.

Business administrators are responsible for managing permissions and ensuring that users receive only the level of access required for their role.

Business administrators should promptly remove access for users who are no longer authorized to access the workspace.

7. Platform Administration and Support Access

SamDlight OS may maintain a platform administration system for service management, security, technical support, billing, and platform operations.

Platform-level administration is logically separated from individual business workspaces. Platform administration data and individual SME business data are not intended to be treated as the same dataset.

Where technical support requires access to a business workspace, access may be controlled through procedures designed to limit the scope and duration of access.

Administrative and support activities may be recorded in audit logs for security, accountability, and operational purposes.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, misuse, or destruction.

Depending on the applicable system and infrastructure, these measures may include:

  • Authentication controls
  • Role-based access controls
  • Secure communications
  • Database access controls
  • Activity logging
  • Security monitoring
  • Backup procedures
  • Infrastructure security controls
  • Administrative security procedures

No internet transmission, software system, database, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain accounts and business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, enforce agreements, and protect legitimate business interests.

When information is no longer required, we may delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal, security, backup, and operational requirements.

Information removed from active systems may remain temporarily in secure backup systems as part of normal disaster-recovery procedures.

10. Account Closure and Data Deletion

Users may request closure of their SamDlight OS account or deletion of eligible personal information.

Depending on applicable law and operational requirements, information may be retained where necessary for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security purposes
  • Financial or transaction obligations
  • Dispute resolution
  • Enforcement of agreements
  • Secure backup and disaster recovery

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SamDlight OS may use cookies, local storage, analytics technologies, and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain login sessions
  • Remember user preferences
  • Improve platform performance
  • Understand product usage
  • Monitor security
  • Improve the user experience

You may configure your browser to restrict or reject certain cookies. Some essential platform functionality may not operate correctly if necessary technologies are disabled.

12. Third-Party Services and Links

SamDlight OS may contain integrations with or links to third-party services.

Third-party providers operate according to their own terms, policies, and privacy practices. We are not responsible for privacy practices outside our control.

13. International Data Processing

SamDlight OS may use cloud infrastructure and technology providers located in different countries.

As a result, information may be processed or stored outside Nigeria or outside the country where a user accesses the Service.

Where applicable, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that international transfers and processing are conducted in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Accessing personal information we hold about you
  • Correcting inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion of eligible information
  • Requesting restriction of certain processing
  • Objecting to certain processing
  • Withdrawing consent where applicable
  • Requesting information about how your data is processed
  • Requesting a copy of certain personal information
  • Opting out of certain promotional communications

Some rights may be subject to legal or legitimate exceptions.

15. Marketing Communications

We may send important operational communications including:

  • Security alerts
  • Account notifications
  • Transaction notifications
  • System updates
  • Support responses
  • Important service announcements

Where permitted by law, we may also send promotional communications. You may unsubscribe from non-essential marketing communications using the unsubscribe mechanism included in the relevant communication.

16. Children's Privacy

SamDlight OS is designed for businesses, entrepreneurs, organizations, and their authorized personnel.

The Service is not intentionally directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

17. Information About Other Individuals

Businesses may use SamDlight OS to store information relating to customers, employees, suppliers, contractors, or other individuals.

If you provide personal information relating to another individual, you are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate lawful basis and any required permissions or notices necessary to provide that information to us.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, business practices, or legal obligations change.

Significant changes may be communicated through appropriate channels where required.

19. Contact SamDlight

If you have questions, concerns, complaints, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the way SamDlight OS processes information, please contact SamDlight Consulting Services through the official SamDlight OS platform.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to remove, restrict, or limit a legal right that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.
