Getting Started

Create your business

Sign up with your email, then create your business workspace — name, currency, and country. You get a 14-day free trial, no card required.

Set up your workspace

Add your first products, record a sale or expense, and get familiar with the dashboard. Everything updates in real time as you go.

Invite staff

From the Team page, share your business's invite code so staff can join directly, or invite them by email. Assign each person a role — owner, manager, or sales.

Configure business settings

Under Settings, set your business logo, currency, and other workspace preferences.

Managing Your Business

Sales & Transactions

Record sales as they happen — single product or multiple items in one transaction. Track payment status (Paid/Pending) and payment method.

Products & Inventory

Add products with cost, price, stock level, and a reorder point. Stock decreases automatically as sales are recorded, and you'll be warned before you oversell.

Customers

Every sale can be linked to a customer. See purchase history, total spend, and visit frequency, with automatic flags for customers at risk of churning.

Expenses & Cash Flow

Log expenses by category and payment method. Cash Flow shows money in vs. money out for any period, calculated from your real records.

Understanding Your Numbers

Profit & Loss

Revenue, cost of goods sold, gross profit, operating expenses, and net profit — calculated automatically from your sales and expenses, no manual bookkeeping required.

Business Reports

Generate a Verified Report for any date range — a shareable, tamper-evident snapshot of your business performance, useful for funding applications.

Business Intelligence

Your dashboard's Business Health Score is calculated from your own real data — revenue trend, profitability, cash position, customer retention, inventory health, growth, and team activity.

AI Business Advisor

What SamDlight AI does

Ask plain-language questions about your business and get answers grounded in your own recorded data — sales, expenses, customers, and inventory.

How it uses business data

It reads your last 90 days of records, summarizes them, and answers your question from that summary — it never sees other businesses' data.

How to ask useful questions

  • "How much did I make this week?"
  • "Which products are my best sellers?"
  • "Why did my profit margin drop last month?"

AI limitations

SamDlight AI answers questions and surfaces insights — it does not take actions on your behalf, send messages, or run automated workflows. If it doesn't have enough data to answer accurately, it says so rather than guessing.

Account & Billing

Plans

Starter, Business, and Professional are self-serve monthly plans. Custom/Enterprise plans are arranged directly with our team.

Upgrading

Upgrade from Settings → Billing. Once payment is verified, your plan activates within seconds.

Payments

Payments are processed securely through Paystack.

Cancellation

See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for details on how cancellation and billing periods work.

Staff accounts

Each plan includes a set number of staff seats. Owners can add or remove staff at any time from the Team page, up to the plan limit.
