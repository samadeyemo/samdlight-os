Help Center

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about your account, business data, and billing.

Account

Double-check the email and password you signed up with. If you're still stuck, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen to reset it.

On the sign-in screen, click "Forgot password" and follow the reset link sent to your email.

Go to Settings → Business Profile to update your business name, currency, country, and logo.

Business

Go to Products, click "+ Add Product", and fill in the name, category, price, cost, and stock level.

Go to Sales, click "+ Record Sale", choose the product(s), customer, and payment status, then save.

Open the transaction from the Sales or Expenses list and edit or delete it. Corrections are reflected immediately in your reports.

Payments

Upgrades activate automatically within seconds of a verified payment. If it's been longer than a few minutes, contact support with your payment reference.

See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for the full process.

Reports

Cost of Goods Sold is the cost price of each product multiplied by the quantity sold, summed across your paid sales for the period.

Net Profit = Revenue − COGS − Operating Expenses. Gross Profit = Revenue − COGS, shown before expenses are subtracted.

Cash Flow compares money actually received (paid sales) against money actually spent (expenses) for the period you select.
