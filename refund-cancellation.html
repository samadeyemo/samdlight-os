Refund & Cancellation Policy

How subscriptions, cancellations, and refunds work on SamDlight OS.

1. Free Trial

New businesses get a 14-day free trial with no card required. You can explore SamDlight OS during this period at no cost, and there is nothing to cancel if you decide not to continue — your trial simply ends.

2. Subscription Billing

Paid plans (Starter, Business, Professional) are billed monthly. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period unless you cancel before it renews. Custom/Enterprise plans are billed according to terms agreed directly with our team.

3. Cancelling Your Subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time from Settings → Billing, or by contacting our support team. When you cancel:

  • You keep access to your paid plan until the end of your current billing period
  • Your subscription will not renew or be charged again after that
  • Your business data remains accessible during the remainder of your paid period

4. What Happens When a Subscription Ends

When your billing period ends without renewal, your business account moves to a restricted state — your data is preserved, but access to paid-plan features is limited until you resubscribe or the account is deleted per our data retention practices (see our Privacy Policy).

5. Refund Eligibility

Because SamDlight OS offers a free trial period to evaluate the product before paying, subscription payments are generally non-refundable once a billing period has started. We may consider refunds on a case-by-case basis in situations such as:

  • A duplicate or accidental charge
  • A verified technical issue on our end that prevented you from using the service you paid for
  • A billing error

To request a refund, contact us within 7 days of the charge via our Contact page, including your business name and payment reference.

6. Plan Upgrades

When you upgrade your plan, the new plan activates immediately after payment is verified. Upgrade payments are not refundable, but you retain the benefits of your new plan for the billing period you paid for.

7. Plan Downgrades

If you downgrade to a lower plan, the change takes effect at the start of your next billing period. You keep your current plan's features until then.

8. Involuntary Cancellation

We may suspend or cancel a subscription if a payment remains unsuccessful after reasonable attempts to resolve the issue, or if an account is found to have materially violated our Terms of Service.

Where appropriate, we may provide reasonable notice or an opportunity to resolve the issue before restricting or cancelling the subscription.

9. Data Retention After Cancellation

When a subscription is cancelled or expires, we do not immediately delete the business account or its records solely because the subscription has ended. Business data may remain retained for a reasonable period to allow the business owner to resubscribe or request access to their information, subject to our Privacy Policy.

After the applicable retention period, we may delete or anonymize account and business data in accordance with our data retention practices and applicable legal requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our billing practices evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about billing, cancellation, or refunds? Reach out via our Contact page.
